ApexDash 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

ApexDash 3D is a digital dashboard for motorcycles and cars, including an Android Auto screen. This policy explains what the app uses and why. In short: the app has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Your data stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

Location

  • The app uses your phone's GPS to show your speed and to record your rides (route, speed, time). It can keep doing this while the screen is off, during a ride, through a visible notification.
  • Recorded rides are saved only on your phone. They are not uploaded to us.
  • To show maps and place or road names, the app sends map positions to these map services: Google Map Tiles, Esri ArcGIS (satellite images), OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (place names). For the local weather it sends your approximate location to Open-Meteo. These services receive the position needed for the request, under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

  • If you turn it on, the app reads incoming notifications (for example messages) only to show them on the dashboard while you ride.
  • Notification content is shown on the screen and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Photos and videos

  • If you allow it, the app reads photos and videos taken during a ride, only to place them in the ride movie that the app makes on your phone.
  • Your photos, videos and ride movies are not uploaded to us.

Bluetooth

The app can connect to an OBD-II Bluetooth adapter to read vehicle data (such as RPM and coolant temperature). This data is shown on the dashboard and used on the phone only.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.

Children

The app is meant for drivers and riders. It is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

All app data is stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
